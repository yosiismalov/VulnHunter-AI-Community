# Authorized Use Only

VulnHunter-AI is intended only for:

- Assets you own.
- Assets you have explicit permission to test.
- Authorized bug bounty programs within their published scope.
- Defensive security research and education.

The user is responsible for reviewing the target program's scope, rules,
rate limits, prohibited testing methods, and data-handling requirements.

VulnHunter-AI identifies research leads and technical signals.
Its output does not prove that a vulnerability exists.

Do not submit automated findings without manual validation and a reproducible
proof of concept.

The author is not responsible for unauthorized, illegal, abusive, or
out-of-scope use.
